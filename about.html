About

About the Archive

Purpose

A guide to Sudanese music

The Sudan Music Archive is a digital guide to Sudanese artists of the twentieth century. It is written for people who want to learn about the music, its history, and the artists who shaped it.

Biographies

How the biographies are written

Each biography lists its sources at the bottom of the page. When a source is a link, you can open it to check the details yourself. If you find an error, or know a source that should be added, please tell us.

Music

Where the music comes from

The archive does not host audio files. Each "Listen on YouTube" button links to an existing upload on YouTube. Our aim is to point visitors to recordings that are published by the artist, a family member, or a label.
