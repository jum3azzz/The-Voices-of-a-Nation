Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Who we are

The operator of this site

Sudan Music Archive is a free digital guide to Sudanese artists of the twentieth century. It is run by Jum3azzz from Sudan. For any question about this policy, write to jum3azzz3333@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you send us

The suggestion form asks for two things: an optional name and a message about an artist. We store only what you type into those two fields. We do not ask for your address, phone number, or date of birth.

Automatic information

What the browser and services see

When you open this site, your browser contacts our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, and the font service, Google Fonts. These services receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type, as part of loading the page. The site does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

The site stores no tracking cookies.

Why we use it

How the information is used

We use suggestions to decide which artists to add to the archive, and to correct biographies. We do not sell information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Who receives the data

Suggestions are stored in a cloud database in the European Union, run by a third-party provider. The website files are hosted by GitHub Pages. Google Fonts and YouTube receive data only through your own use of their services, as described above. We share data with these providers only to run the site.

How long we keep it

Retention

We keep a suggestion until we add the artist, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to jum3azzz3333@gmail.com and include the text of your suggestion, so we can find it. We reply as soon as we reasonably can, and usually within 30 days.

Do not send sensitive personal information through the suggestion form.

Children

Visitors under 18

The site is not made for children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
